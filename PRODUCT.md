## Platform
web
## Stack
Delegated within the supplied options: Vite + React, hash routes, IndexedDB.
## Product Purpose
OPC之路 × TONK is a personal dream growth journal. Five screens let a person record dreams, daily journals, ideas and monthly progress from their phone.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Dream CRUD, images and progress; dated journals with four prompts, images and dream link; idea CRUD, tags, search and chronological sorting; local monthly statistics. No backend, accounts, cloud, social, payments or AI APIs. GitHub Pages compatible. Future drag, resize and rotate metadata only.
## Brand Commitments
Supplied TONK logo, character and four-screen concept are binding visual references. Characters decorate; personal content leads. Chinese interface.
## Evidence on Hand
Three original PNG assets supplied by the user. All seeded records are illustrative demo data.
## Product Principles
Simple, stable, beautiful, usable. Mobile first, bounded desktop content. Central data access. No expanded scope.
